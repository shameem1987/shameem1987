## 👋 About Me

I lead engineering teams that build **enterprise-scale, cloud-native SaaS platforms**. I combine technical leadership with AI-assisted software engineering to deliver faster, with higher quality and reliability, using **AI to amplify engineering judgment, not replace it**.

Over **16+ years** I've grown from software developer to engineering leader, delivering products across **Supply Chain, Healthcare and Air Traffic Management**. Today I lead a distributed team of **8 engineers across India and the US**, building a **Supply Chain Planning & Cost Modeling platform** that supports **USD 50M+ ARR** with **99.9% production availability**.

- 🤖 Built a **self-healing Playwright test agent** (LangChain, LangGraph, RAG)
- 🧠 Led adoption of **Claude Code, GPT-5 and Gemini** across the SDLC
- 🧰 Creator of **[SwissUtils](https://swissutils.com/)**, an AI-powered platform with 100+ developer productivity tools
- 📖 Author of ***The AI Engineering Lead Playbook***, a practical guide for leaders building AI-native delivery teams
- 💼 Open to **Engineering Manager** opportunities leading distributed teams that build reliable, AI-enabled SaaS products
