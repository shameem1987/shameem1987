- 🚀 Leading engineering teams that deliver reliable, scalable software
- 🤖 Integrating AI into the development lifecycle (code, review, testing, delivery)
- 🏗️ Architecture, platform strategy and technical roadmaps
- 🎯 Bridging business goals and engineering execution
- 🌱 Mentoring engineers and growing future leaders
